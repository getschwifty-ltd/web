Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

TapMania Legacy respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the iOS and Android game and its service providers collect, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game stores your settings, progress, scores, imported content, timing-sync profiles, and purchase entitlement status on your device. Imported song files are not uploaded to us. Some song and difficulty metadata may be included in the analytics events described below when you play.

Analytics Data Collection

TapMania Legacy uses Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase to help us understand how the game is used and to improve performance, gameplay balance, and user experience.

The game may collect analytics data such as:

  • App launches and scene/screen transitions
  • Gameplay start, completion, abandonment, and result summaries
  • Selected gameplay options and settings, such as display mode, scroll speed, note skin, audio settings, haptics settings, and visual settings
  • Song and difficulty metadata used for gameplay analytics
  • Purchase attempts, completions, failures, and remove-ads entitlement events
  • Ad requests and display events, achievement unlocks, and timing-sync activity
  • Device, app, platform, version, and diagnostic information collected automatically by Firebase / Google Analytics

Firebase / Google Analytics may also use identifiers such as an app-instance identifier to distinguish app installations and sessions, as well as information such as device and app information, approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostics. We do not use analytics to collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone input, camera input, or payment card information.

We use this data to understand usage patterns, diagnose issues, and improve the game. Events can be associated with an app-installation identifier; they are not limited to aggregate reports.

Crash Reports and Diagnostics

TapMania Legacy uses Firebase Crashlytics to help us detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and stability issues. Crash reports may include information such as device model, operating system version, app version, crash logs, diagnostic data, and a crash installation identifier.

Crash reports are used to improve app stability and are not used to identify you by name or contact you.

Third-Party Services

TapMania Legacy integrates Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Google AdMob, and Google’s User Messaging Platform, all provided by Google.

These services process data according to Google’s own privacy and data handling practices. You can learn more at: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

TapMania Legacy shows advertisements using Google AdMob unless you have purchased the remove-ads option. Ads may not be available in every session. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, IP address, approximate location, advertising identifiers where available, app activity, diagnostics, and ad interactions to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads.

Where permitted and where you consent, ads may be personalized. Otherwise, Google may serve non-personalized or limited ads when its consent status permits ad requests. If you authorize tracking on iOS, Google and its advertising partners may link data from this app with activity from other companies’ apps or websites for ad targeting and measurement.

More information about Google advertising technologies and AdMob is available at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Consent Choices

In regions where consent is required, TapMania Legacy uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for and manage your advertising choices. Your choices may be stored on your device and may be communicated to Google and its partners so they can apply your selected preferences.

On iOS, the game may also ask for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before an advertising identifier can be used for tracking. You can change that permission in iOS Settings. In the game, open Options to view the Privacy Policy and, when required, the Ad Choices form. Analytics and crash reporting are disabled while a required consent decision is unresolved and may remain disabled depending on that decision.

Game Center and Play Games

The game does not create a TapMania account. If you use platform achievements, Apple Game Center or Google Play Games handles sign-in and achievement reporting. The game stores your platform player ID locally so it can associate pending achievement awards with the right account. We do not send that player ID to Firebase Analytics.

In-App Purchases

TapMania Legacy offers a one-time purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by the applicable platform provider, such as Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

The game checks purchase status with the platform store to unlock the feature and stores the entitlement state on your device. If analytics collection is enabled, purchase-flow events may include the product identifier and outcome.

Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it through the game where that option is available or uninstall the app. Analytics and crash data is processed and retained by Google under the settings and retention periods for those services. Platform purchase and achievement records are managed by Apple or Google. To ask about data we control or request deletion where we can identify the relevant records, email getschwifty.ltd@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to locate the records.

Permissions

TapMania Legacy does not request access to your camera, contacts, microphone, or precise location.

On some platforms, the game may use platform capabilities such as haptics/vibration for gameplay feedback and file/document access for importing supported song packages.

Children’s Privacy

TapMania Legacy is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete or address the information.

Data Security

Analytics data transmitted by Firebase / Google Analytics is protected in transit using standard security measures such as HTTPS/TLS.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

This policy is hosted at: https://getschwifty.ltd/privacy

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this privacy policy, contact us at: getschwifty.ltd@gmail.com

If you email us, we use your address and message to respond to your request and keep the correspondence as needed to handle it.
