Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

TapMania Legacy respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the game collects, how it is used, and what third-party services are included.

Analytics Data Collection

TapMania Legacy uses Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase to help us understand how the game is used and to improve stability, performance, gameplay balance, and user experience.

The game may collect analytics data such as:

  • App launches and scene/screen transitions
  • Gameplay start, completion, abandonment, and result summaries
  • Selected gameplay options and settings, such as display mode, scroll speed, note skin, audio settings, haptics settings, and visual settings
  • Song and difficulty metadata used for gameplay analytics
  • Purchase-flow analytics events if purchase functionality is enabled in a future version
  • Device, app, platform, version, and diagnostic information collected automatically by Firebase / Google Analytics

Firebase / Google Analytics may also use identifiers such as an app-instance identifier to distinguish app installations and sessions. We do not use analytics to collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone input, camera input, or payment card information.

Analytics data is used only to understand aggregate usage patterns, diagnose issues, and improve the game.

Third-Party Services

TapMania Legacy integrates Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google.

Firebase / Google Analytics processes analytics data according to Google’s own privacy and data handling practices. You can learn more at: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Advertising

TapMania Legacy does not currently display ads.

The game does not currently use advertising networks to show banner ads, interstitial ads, rewarded ads, or personalized ads.

If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before or at the time that change is released.

In-App Purchases

TapMania Legacy may include code support for in-app purchases, but the current version does not expose an in-app purchase flow to users.

If purchases are enabled in a future version, purchases will be processed by the applicable platform provider, such as Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Permissions

TapMania Legacy does not request access to your camera, contacts, microphone, or precise location.

On some platforms, the game may use platform capabilities such as haptics/vibration for gameplay feedback and file/document access for importing supported song packages.

Children’s Privacy

TapMania Legacy is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete or address the information.

Data Security

Analytics data transmitted by Firebase / Google Analytics is protected in transit using standard security measures such as HTTPS/TLS.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

This policy is hosted at: https://getschwifty.ltd/privacy.html
